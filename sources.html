By chapter

01 · Chaos & fractals

  • Adilson Motter and David Campbell — Chaos at fifty (Physics Today, 2013) — the history of chaos and its reach since Lorenz.
  • Jos Leys, Étienne Ghys and Aurélien Alvarez — Chaos — a free film in nine chapters, from Newton’s laws to the butterfly effect and Smale’s horseshoe.
  • Edward Lorenz — Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow (1963) — the original paper, still readable.
  • Jacques Laskar — Is the Solar System Stable? — chaos and stability of the planets, from Laplace to the present.
  • Predrag Cvitanović et al. — Chaos: Classical and Quantum — a free online textbook.
  • David Avnir et al. — Is the geometry of nature fractal? (Science, 1998; a longer free version is The limited scaling range of empirical fractals) — how far fractal scaling reaches in experiments.
  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures — Julia sets, chaos on them, and the history of the Mandelbrot set.

02 · Mathematical foundations

  • Chris Lipa, Cornell Math Explorers’ Club — One-Dimensional Maps — iteration, fixed points, cobweb diagrams and periodic orbits.
  • Robert Devaney — The fundamental dichotomy — the role of the critical orbit in quadratic connectedness.
  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures — rigorous foundations and local dynamics; later expanded into the book of the same name (third edition, Princeton University Press, 2006).

03 · Orbits & stability

  • Robert Devaney — Role of the Critical Orbit: the connectedness criterion and attracting-cycle theorem.
  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures: attracting, repelling, parabolic, and irrationally indifferent dynamics; the theorems of Kœnigs (6.1) and Böttcher (6.7).
  • Mikhail Lyubich — Conformal Geometry and Dynamics of Quadratic Polynomials: the theorem that there is at most one non-repelling cycle, Theorem 28.13 (book draft; a 35 MB PDF).

04 · Julia sets

  • Mikhail Lyubich — Conformal Geometry and Dynamics of Quadratic Polynomials — filled Julia sets, Julia boundaries, and the quadratic connectivity theorem, especially §20; Julia sets that are quasicircles, Proposition 25.21 (book draft; a 35 MB PDF).
  • Robert Devaney — The Mandelbrot Set Explorer, Exploration #3 — a visual introduction to how parameters select connected sets or dust.
  • Omar Antolín Camarena — Iteration of Rational Functions — Julia and Fatou sets, periodic components, and normal families.
  • Christopher Bishop — Wandering domains — a detailed exposition of Sullivan’s theorem, with a proof sketch for polynomials.
  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures — the structure of Julia sets (§3, §11), the filled Julia set and connectivity (§17), and the local theory.

05 · The Mandelbrot set

  • Chris Lipa, Cornell Math Explorers’ Club — The Mandelbrot Set — the parameter-plane and Julia-set connection.
  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — Itération des polynômes quadratiques complexes — the 1982 note proving that M is connected (in French).
  • Kathryn Lindsey — An Introduction to the Mandelbrot and Julia Sets — compactness, connectedness, and external coordinates.
  • Curtis McMullen — Complex Dynamics and Renormalization — stability and the boundary of M as bifurcation locus (§4.1–4.2), and the open conjectures (§1.2).
  • John Milnor — Periodic orbits, external rays and the Mandelbrot set: an expository account — a deeper account of the organization of periodic features.
  • Dzmitry Dudko — On the MLC Conjecture and the Renormalization Theory in Complex Dynamics — research context and open problems; to appear in the Proceedings of the ICM 2026.
  • Dierk Schleicher — On Fibers and Local Connectivity of Mandelbrot and Multibrot Sets — local connectivity and density of hyperbolicity.

06 · A short history

  • Michèle Audin — Fatou, Julia, Montel: The Great Prize of Mathematical Sciences of 1918, and Beyond — the archival history of the prize and its background (Springer, 2011; the author’s free English edition).
  • Gaston Julia — Mémoire sur l’itération des fonctions rationnelles (1918) and Pierre Fatou, Sur les équations fonctionnelles (1919–1920) — the two founding memoirs.
  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures — Appendix F gives a short, balanced account of the discovery of the Mandelbrot set.
  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — Itération des polynômes quadratiques complexes (1982) and the Orsay notes in English — the founding theorems.
  • Adrien Douady — Systèmes dynamiques holomorphes, Séminaire Bourbaki 1982 — the first survey of the reborn field, with the question of local connectivity.
  • John Horgan — Mandelbrot Set-To, Scientific American, 1990 — the priority dispute in the participants’ words.
  • Gazette des Mathématiciens 113 (2007) — Hommage à Adrien Douady — recollections by Hubbard, Tan Lei, Lyubich and others.
  • Jean-René Chazottes and Marc Monticelli — Computer experiments and visualization in mathematics and physics — how the computer pictures came about.

07 · Cardioids, bulbs & periods

  • Robert Devaney — Periods of the Bulbs, and rotation numbers and Farey organization.
  • Mikhail Lyubich — Conformal Geometry and Dynamics of Quadratic Polynomials, §33 (the parameter plane) and §35 (hyperbolic components); a book draft, 35 MB PDF.
  • John Milnor — Periodic orbits, external rays and the Mandelbrot set: an expository account.
  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — Exploring the Mandelbrot set: the Orsay notes, with hyperbolic components (chapter 14) and Gleason’s lemma (chapter 19).
  • Robert Devaney — The Mandelbrot set, the Farey tree, and the Fibonacci sequence (American Mathematical Monthly, 1999).
  • Curtis McMullen — Complex Dynamics and Renormalization, §1.2: density of hyperbolicity and related conjectures.

08 · External rays & angles

  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures: Böttcher’s theorem (6.7), the potential and the filled Julia set (§17), external rays and periodic points (§18).
  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — Exploring the Mandelbrot set: the Orsay notes: the map ΦM (Theorem 8.1), Misiurewicz points (Theorem 8.2), landing of rational rays (Theorem 13.1).
  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — Itération des polynômes quadratiques complexes (Comptes Rendus, 1982), the first announcement.
  • John Milnor — Periodic orbits, external rays and the Mandelbrot set: an expository account: orbit portraits, wakes and orbit forcing.
  • Dierk Schleicher — Rational Parameter Rays of the Mandelbrot Set, and Dominik Eberlein, Sabyasachi Mukherjee and Dierk Schleicher — Rational parameter rays of the Multibrot sets: precise landing theorems.
  • Stanisław Domoradzki and Małgorzata Stawiska — Lucjan Emil Böttcher and his mathematical legacy.

09 · Special points

  • Tan Lei — Similarity Between the Mandelbrot Set and Julia Sets (1990) — the original asymptotic similarity result.
  • Tomoki Kawahira — Zalcman functions and similarity between the Mandelbrot set, Julia sets, and the tricorn — a later approach to that theorem.
  • Bodil Branner and Núria Fagella — Homeomorphisms between limbs of the Mandelbrot set (Journal of Geometric Analysis, 1999) — among much else, the main branch point of each limb.
  • Jacek Graczyk and Grzegorz Świątek — Harmonic measure and expansion on the boundary of the connectedness locus (Inventiones mathematicae, 2000), and Stanislav Smirnov — Symbolic dynamics and Collet–Eckmann conditions (International Mathematics Research Notices, 2000) — what a typical point of the boundary is like.
  • Saeed Zakeri — On biaccessible points of the Mandelbrot set (Proceedings of the AMS, 2006) — a typical point of the boundary, in the sense of harmonic measure, has a single parameter ray landing on it.
  • Dzmitry Dudko and Mikhail Lyubich — MLC at Feigenbaum points (Publications mathématiques de l’IHÉS, 2026) — local connectivity at the Feigenbaum point and related parameters.
  • Jeremy Kahn, Alex Kapiamba and Mikhail Lyubich — MLC for parabolically bounded primitive renormalization (2026 preprint, not yet refereed) — recent progress for another class of parameters, not a proof of the full conjecture.

10 · Minibrots & self-similarity

  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — On the dynamics of polynomial-like mappings (1985) — the original straightening and small-copy construction.
  • Mikhail Lyubich — Conformal Geometry and Dynamics of Quadratic Polynomials — detailed theory of renormalization and little copies.
  • Saeed Zakeri — External rays and the real slice of the Mandelbrot set — tuning and periods, with background in section 2.
  • Dzmitry Dudko — The Decoration Theorem for Mandelbrot and Multibrot Sets — the structure of attached decorations.
  • Mikhail Lyubich — Feigenbaum–Coullet–Tresser universality and Milnor’s Hairiness Conjecture — rigorous renormalization and universality theory.

11 · Embedded Julia sets

  • Robert Munafo — Embedded Julia Set, Mu-Ency — the name, the types and many locations; see also the entries Bifurcation and Julia Morphing.
  • Tomoki Kawahira and Masashi Kisaka — Julia sets appear quasiconformally in the Mandelbrot set (arXiv, 2018, revised 2024) — the theorems; part II (2025) gives a parabolic proof.
  • Adrien Douady, Xavier Buff, Robert Devaney and Pierre Sentenac — Baby Mandelbrot sets are born in cauliflowers (2000) — the first proof, near the cusp of a minibrot.
  • Wolf Jung — Renormalization and embedded Julia sets in the Mandelbrot set (draft, 2020) — levels, nodes and their scaling.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Two spirals out (2015) and Patterns in embedded Julia sets (2017) — Julia morphing and the depths of its levels.
  • Tan Lei — Similarity Between the Mandelbrot Set and Julia Sets (1990) — the resemblance at Misiurewicz points.

12 · Neutral cycles

  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures: the Leau–Fatou flower (§7), Cremer points and Siegel disks with the theorems of Siegel, Brjuno and Yoccoz (§8), and Cremer points and local connectivity (§18).
  • Carl Ludwig Siegel — Iteration of analytic functions (Annals of Mathematics, 1942).
  • Jean-Christophe Yoccoz — Théorème de Siegel, nombres de Bruno et polynômes quadratiques (Astérisque 231, 1995).
  • Adrien Douady — Disques de Siegel et anneaux de Herman (Séminaire Bourbaki, 1987).
  • Adrien Douady — L’ensemble de Julia dépend-il continûment du polynôme ? (Journées X-UPS, 1994): continuity of Julia sets and parabolic implosion.
  • Xavier Buff and Jasmin Raissy — Introduction to Fatou components in holomorphic dynamics: Fatou coordinates and Lavaurs maps.
  • Carsten Lunde Petersen — Local connectivity of some Julia sets containing a circle with an irrational rotation (Acta Mathematica, 1996), and Petersen and Saeed Zakeri — On the Julia set of a typical quadratic polynomial with a Siegel disk (Annals of Mathematics, 2004).
  • Aaron Klebanoff — π in the Mandelbrot set (Fractals, 2001).

13 · Ghost bulbs

  • Adrien Douady — L’ensemble de Julia dépend-il continûment du polynôme ? (Journées X-UPS, 1994) — the phase, Lavaurs maps, the “another chance” mechanism (§§17–19), the limit picture near 1/4 with Lavaurs’ theorem and Douady’s conjecture (§23), other parabolic points (§24). In English in Complex Dynamical Systems, Proc. Sympos. Appl. Math. 49 (AMS, 1994).
  • Arnaud Chéritat — Parabolic implosion: a mini-course (lecture notes, 2011) — Fatou coordinates, the phase and Lavaurs’ theorem for any parabolic point.
  • John Milnor — Periodic orbits, external rays and the Mandelbrot set: an expository account (Astérisque 261, 2000) — Lemma 4.5: the multiplier as a coordinate at a root.
  • Alex Kapiamba — An optimal Yoccoz inequality for near-parabolic quadratic polynomials (arXiv, 2021, revised 2025) — §3, escaping sets of Lavaurs maps, counted by passages, with a picture of the phase plane at the cusp.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Stretching cusps (2013) — valleys straightened so that their bulbs all have about the same size.
  • Robert Munafo — Seahorse Valley and Double Spiral Valley, Mu-Ency — the names of the two sides.

15 · Area, dimension & numbers

  • Daniel Bittner, Long Cheong, Dante Gates and Hieu Nguyen — New approximations for the area of the Mandelbrot set (Involve, 2017): the Laurent series, Gronwall’s area theorem and the history of the bounds.
  • John Ewing and Glenn Schober — The area of the Mandelbrot set (Numerische Mathematik, 1992).
  • Thorsten Förstemann — Numerical estimation of the area of the Mandelbrot set (2012).
  • Mitsuhiro Shishikura — The Hausdorff dimension of the boundary of the Mandelbrot set and Julia sets (Annals of Mathematics, 1998).
  • Curtis McMullen — Hausdorff dimension and conformal dynamics III: Computation of dimension (American Journal of Mathematics, 1998).
  • Thies Brockmoeller, Oscar Scherz and Nedim Srkalovic — Pi in the Mandelbrot set everywhere (preprint, 2025), with the history of the π phenomenon.
  • Aaron Klebanoff — π in the Mandelbrot set (Fractals, 2001).
  • Gerald Edgar — Pi and the Mandelbrot set: Boll’s discovery, with a link to his 1991 post, and the first explanations.
  • Thorsten Förstemann — Numerical estimation of the area of the Mandelbrot set using quad tree tessellation and distance estimation (2017).

16 · Beyond z² + c

  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures: rational maps, critical points and attracting cycles, the classification of Fatou components, and Herman rings.
  • Dominik Eberlein, Sabyasachi Mukherjee and Dierk Schleicher — Rational parameter rays of the Multibrot sets: roots, co-roots and the landing theorems in every degree.
  • John Hubbard and Dierk Schleicher — Multicorns are not path connected, with a survey of the antiholomorphic family in its introduction.
  • John Milnor — Remarks on iterated cubic maps (Experimental Mathematics, 1992): cubic parameter spaces and the tricorn.
  • Curtis McMullen — The Mandelbrot set is universal (in The Mandelbrot Set, Theme and Variations, 2000).
  • John Hubbard, Dierk Schleicher and Scott Sutherland — How to find all roots of complex polynomials by Newton’s method (Inventiones, 2001).
  • John Milnor — On Lattès maps.
  • Walter Bergweiler — Iteration of meromorphic functions (Bulletin of the AMS, 1993): the transcendental case.

17 · Angles, addresses & trees

  • Dierk Schleicher — Internal addresses in the Mandelbrot set and Galois groups of polynomials: kneading sequences, internal and angled internal addresses.
  • John Milnor — Periodic orbits, external rays and the Mandelbrot set: an expository account: orbit portraits, wakes and the landing of rays.
  • Adrien Douady and John Hubbard — Exploring the Mandelbrot set: the Orsay notes: Hubbard trees and the combinatorics of rays.
  • John Hubbard and Dierk Schleicher — The spider algorithm (in Complex Dynamical Systems, Proceedings of Symposia in Applied Mathematics 49).
  • Henk Bruin, Alexandra Kaffl and Dierk Schleicher — Existence of quadratic Hubbard trees (Fundamenta Mathematicae, 2009).
  • William Thurston — On the geometry and dynamics of iterated rational maps (in Complex Dynamics: Families and Friends, edited by Dierk Schleicher, A K Peters, 2009, pp. 3–130).
  • Saeed Zakeri — External rays and the real slice of the Mandelbrot set: the abstract Mandelbrot set and what it captures.
  • Giulio Tiozzo — Continuity of core entropy of quadratic polynomials (Inventiones, 2016).

18 · Open questions

  • Dzmitry Dudko — On the MLC Conjecture and the Renormalization Theory in Complex Dynamics (to appear in the Proceedings of the ICM 2026).
  • Anna Miriam Benini — A survey on MLC, rigidity and related topics.
  • John Hubbard — Local connectivity of Julia sets and bifurcation loci: three theorems of J.-C. Yoccoz (1993).
  • John Milnor — Local connectivity of Julia sets: expository lectures.
  • Dierk Schleicher — On Fibers and Local Connectivity of Mandelbrot and Multibrot Sets.
  • Curtis McMullen — Complex Dynamics and Renormalization (1994): the conjectures and their equivalent forms.
  • Mikhail Lyubich — Conformal Geometry and Dynamics of Quadratic Polynomials: puzzles, parapuzzles and renormalization in detail.

19 · Computing the pictures

  • John Milnor — Dynamics in One Complex Variable, introductory lectures, Appendix G, “Remarks on computer graphics”.
  • Heinz-Otto Peitgen and Dietmar Saupe (editors) — The Science of Fractal Images (Springer, 1988), with the algorithms LSM, CPM, BDM, DEM, IIM and MIIM, and Yuval Fisher’s appendix.
  • Linas Vepstas — Renormalizing the Mandelbrot escape (1997): the smooth count.
  • Jussi Härkönen — On Smooth Fractal Coloring Techniques (master’s thesis, Åbo Akademi University, 2007): average colorings, the triangle inequality and curvature averages, and the stripe average.
  • Javier Barrallo and Damien M. Jones — Coloring Algorithms for Dynamical Systems in the Complex Plane (Visual Mathematics, vol. 1, no. 4, 1999), with orbit traps.
  • Guillaume Brouillette, Pierre-Olivier Parisé and Dominic Rochon — Tricomplex distance estimation for filled-in Julia sets and Multibrot sets (2019), with a proof of the two-sided bound in every degree.
  • Dietmar Saupe — Efficient computation of Julia sets and their fractal dimension (Physica D, 1987).
  • Dierk Schleicher and Robin Stoll — Newton’s method in practice: finding all roots of polynomials of degree one million efficiently (2017).
  • Mark Braverman and Michael Yampolsky — Computability of Julia Sets (Springer, 2009).

20 · Deep zoom

  • K. I. Martin — SuperFractalThing Maths (2013, archived) — the two-page note that introduced perturbation and series approximation.
  • Pauldelbrot — Pertubation Theory Glitches Improvement (fractalforums.com, April 2014) — the analysis and detection of glitches, with replies by the authors of other programs.
  • Zhuoran — Another solution to perturbation glitches (fractalforums.org, August 2021, archived) — rebasing, and the discussion of periodic references that followed.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Deep zoom theory and practice (2021) — number formats, rescaling, glitch tests and series approximation.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Deep zoom theory and practice (again) (2022) — rebasing and bilinear approximation.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Deriving the size estimate (2016) — the size and orientation of a minibrot from its nucleus orbit.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Atom domains and Newton basins (2021) — periods from new minima of |⁠zn|, and why Newton’s starting points need care.
  • Claude Heiland-Allen — Kalles Fraktaler 2 + manual — number formats by depth, glitch correction and reference options in practice.
  • Matthew Renzelmann — FractalShark — source code with extensive notes on precision, BLA, LA, compression and graphics processors.
  • Zhuoran — Imagina — source code of linear approximation with stages and reference compression.
  • GMP manual — Multiplication Algorithms — why long numbers are expensive, and which method is used when.
